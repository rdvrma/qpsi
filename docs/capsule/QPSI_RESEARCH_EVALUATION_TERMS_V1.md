## 1. Purpose & Scope of Evaluation

The Q-Psi Research Capsule provides remote, support-gated evaluation access to the proprietary Q-Psi State-Space Compiler. These terms govern all evaluation sessions, workload submissions, and telemetry data generated through the Research Capsule.

---

## 2. Pre-Existing Intellectual Property Clause

The Q-Psi State-Space Compiler, its pre-existing implementation, architecture, internal tooling and associated proprietary technology remain the pre-existing intellectual property of their applicable rights holder(s). Research Evaluation access does not assign ownership, authorship, copyright, patent rights, source-code rights, or commercialization rights to the researcher.

Researcher results, feedback, benchmark observations and independently created research remain attributable according to the applicable research agreement; use of the Capsule does not make the researcher an author or creator of the pre-existing Q-Psi compiler.

---

## 3. License Grant and Restrictions

1. **Non-Exclusive Evaluation**: A Research Evaluation License grants a limited, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to submit eligible workloads for remote compiler evaluation.
2. **No Source-Code Access**: Evaluation access does not provide or imply access to proprietary compiler source code, internal transformation passes, or raw model weights.
3. **No Commercial Rights**: Research Evaluation Licenses do not permit commercial production deployment, software integration, commercial reselling, or sublicensing. Commercial arrangements require a separate commercial licensing agreement.
4. **No Decompilation or Probing**: Reverse engineering, timing side-channel attacks, or extraction of proprietary compiler internals is strictly prohibited.

---

## 4. Attribution and Publication Boundary

1. **Independent Research Attribution**: Researchers are free to publish academic findings, empirical observations, and benchmark comparisons derived from their use of the Research Capsule, provided they cite the Q-Psi compiler accurately.
2. **Authorship Boundary**: Use of the Research Capsule does NOT confer authorship, co-authorship, or inventorship of the underlying Q-Psi compiler or its existing codebase.
3. **Joint Contributions**: If an external researcher collaborates on novel, joint scientific research beyond standard evaluation, paper authorship and technical IP boundaries must be governed by a separate written agreement prior to integration.

---

## 5. Fail-Closed Acknowledgment

All issued Research Evaluation Licenses require active acknowledgment of these Pre-Existing IP Terms (`acknowledged_preexisting_ip = true`). Licenses without verified acknowledgment will fail closed and cannot authenticate Capsule sessions.
